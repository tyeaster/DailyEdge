# Glossary

This glossary defines TrueLine product, betting, data, and engineering terms.

## Betting Terms

### American Odds

Odds format such as `-120` or `+150`. Negative odds show how much must be risked to win 100. Positive odds show how much is won on a 100 stake.

### Implied Probability

The probability implied by odds.

### Vig

Sportsbook margin embedded in odds.

### No-Vig Probability

Market probability after removing sportsbook margin from a two-sided market.

### Fair Line

The price implied by TrueLine's model probability.

### Edge

Difference between TrueLine model probability and sportsbook implied probability.

### Expected Value

Expected return based on model probability and sportsbook odds.

### Closing Line Value

How much better or worse a placed price is compared to the closing market price.

### Steam Move

A rapid market move, often indicating sharp or coordinated betting activity.

### Reverse Line Movement

Movement against the apparent public side.

## Product Terms

### TrueLine Score

Unified 0-100 recommendation quality score generated by RankingEngine.

### Recommendation

Decision label such as `Pass`, `Lean`, `Play`, `Strong Play`, or `Elite`.

### Confidence

Model certainty. Separate from odds, edge, and EV.

### Data Quality

Score representing completeness and reliability of model inputs.

### Best Bets

Unified ranked board across markets.

### Daily Slate

Main dashboard focused on today's MLB games and betting opportunities.

## Market Terms

### Moneyline

Bet on which team wins the game.

### Run Line

Baseball spread market, commonly plus or minus 1.5 runs.

### Game Total

Over/under market for combined runs in a game.

### Team Total

Over/under market for one team's runs.

### Strikeouts

Player prop market for pitcher strikeouts.

### Hits

Player prop market for hitter hits.

### Total Bases

Player prop market counting singles as 1, doubles as 2, triples as 3, and home runs as 4.

### Home Runs

Player prop market for whether a hitter hits a home run.

## Baseball Data Terms

### OPS

On-base plus slugging.

### ISO

Isolated power, usually slugging minus batting average.

### WHIP

Walks plus hits per inning pitched.

### K/9

Strikeouts per nine innings.

### BB/9

Walks per nine innings.

### HR/9

Home runs allowed per nine innings.

### Barrel %

Percentage of batted balls with ideal exit velocity and launch angle.

### Hard Hit %

Percentage of batted balls hit at high exit velocity.

### Sweet Spot %

Percentage of batted balls in a desirable launch-angle range.

### Pitch Arsenal

The set of pitch types a pitcher throws.

### Pitch Match

Compatibility score between pitcher pitch mix and batter pitch-type performance.

### Zone Match

Compatibility score between pitcher location tendencies and batter zone damage.

## Engineering Terms

### Provider

Interface and implementation that retrieves external or mocked data.

### Live Provider

Provider that calls an external data source.

### Replay Provider

Provider that reads saved fixtures.

### Mock Provider

Provider that returns deterministic synthetic data.

### Service

Business logic layer that consumes normalized data and returns domain outputs or view models.

### View Model

Typed object built for UI rendering.

### Candidate

A bettable opportunity before or after ranking.

### BetCandidate

Shared RankingEngine input model.

### Feature

Product-specific folder under `src/features/`.
