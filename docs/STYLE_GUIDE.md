# DailyEdge Style Guide

DailyEdge should feel like a premium MLB analytics dashboard: fast, precise, restrained, and trustworthy.

## Visual Direction

- Premium sports analytics, not sportsbook promotion.
- Dark navy foundation with crisp contrast.
- Minimal color accents reserved for meaning, status, confidence, and value.
- Dense but readable layouts for scanning today's slate.

## Avoid Sportsbook Cliches

Avoid:

- Casino imagery
- Neon betting language
- Oversized promotional badges
- Flashing urgency patterns
- Generic trophy or ticket motifs
- Loud gradients that distract from data

DailyEdge should feel closer to a financial terminal than a betting splash page.

## Reusable Components

Use shared, typed components for repeated patterns:

- Game cards
- Bet cards
- Prop cards
- Weather cards
- Injury rows
- KPI cards
- Badges
- Confidence indicators

Do not duplicate layout, typography, or card styles when a reusable component can own the pattern.

## Typography

- Prioritize legibility and hierarchy.
- Use compact headings for dashboard sections.
- Keep labels short and consistent.
- Use tabular or stable number styling where available for odds, percentages, and units.
- Avoid oversized hero copy in the product dashboard.

## Card Design

- Cards should be clean, tight, and scannable.
- Use subtle borders and restrained surfaces.
- Keep rounded corners modest.
- Avoid nested cards.
- Make expandable content feel like a continuation of the card, not a new layout system.

## Spacing

- Use consistent spacing scales.
- Keep dashboard density high enough to compare games and bets quickly.
- Preserve enough whitespace for readability.
- Align numbers and labels carefully.

## Mobile-First Principles

- The Daily Slate should work on narrow screens first.
- Prioritize vertical scanning.
- Keep actions reachable and obvious.
- Prevent text clipping and horizontal overflow.
- Collapse dense secondary detail behind expandable cards or sections.

## Interaction Principles

- Fast feedback.
- Clear loading and error states.
- Stable layouts during refreshes.
- No surprise navigation from dashboard cards.
- Refresh and data status should be visible without dominating the screen.
